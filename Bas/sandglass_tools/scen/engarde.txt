' CTRL.S startfall puts the sword away as the fall begins, and for the player
' it also sets droppedout.  The port did neither.
'
' Left drawn, the landing runs softland's crouch and FightCtrl is in charge of
' pose 109, which takes no input: he crouches where he lands until something
' kills him.  Retreating off a ledge is the ordinary way out of a fight, so
' this was a soft-lock in the one move you most want.  And droppedout is what
' tells the guard the kid has gone over the edge; nothing ever set it, so
' FollowKid never ran and nobody ever came down after him.
'
' Level 1 screen 3 is the guard's room and has no ledge in it, so one is made
' under him once the fight is going: the sword-fight script from the coverage
' pass leaves him en garde at column 4, and that floor is then taken away with
' somewhere put below to land on.  How the floor went does not matter - what
' is being tested is what a step-off does to a character with his sword out.
