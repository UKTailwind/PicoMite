' scen/wall.txt - section 3: coming down on a solid block.
'
' CTRL.S falling, at the moment he passes through a floor plane:
'     :1 jsr getunderft
'        cmp #block
'        bne :2
'        jsr InsideBlock ;reset him to either side of block
'     :2 jsr cmpspace
'        bne hitflr
'        inc CharBlockY ;Fall thru floor plane
'
' A solid block is not space, so he lands on it - after InsideBlock has nudged
' him towards whichever side of it is clear.  The port has a solid block in
' its no-floor set, which is right for "nobody can stand inside one" and wrong
' for this, so he fell straight through the wall and out of the room.
'
' A wall is put in the way of a step-off on level 1 screen 1.

SCEN coming down on a wall lands on it
AT 1 1 3 0
TYPE 1 2 1 20
X 105
SEED 17
RUN ..v..........
WANT SCRN 1
WANT BY 1
WANT ALIVE 1
END

' KNOWN DEFECT, recorded rather than fixed.  COLL.S COLLISIONS lets a
' character through a barrier only while he is hanging (action 2 or 6),
' climbing (poses 135-148) or turning (action 7), so the original bumps in the
' air as well as on the ground.  It can afford to: CHECKBARR works from the
' character's own image edges against the barrier's edge - the CD/SN nybble
' buffers and the BarL/BarR tables - and a collision is a nybble that goes
' from 0 to 1 between frames.  This port has none of that, and what stands in
' for it is "the block ahead is a barrier and he has reached its edge".  On
' the ground that is the same answer.  In the air it is not: a man falling
' PAST a wall is beside it every frame, so letting the test run in the air
' smacks him into the wall under every ledge he reaches for and the grab stops
' working altogether.  So HitBarrier still leaves the air alone.
'
' Level 1 screen 1 has floor at columns 3 to 7 and solid block at 8 and 9.  A
' running jump from column 3 meets that wall at the top of its arc, nineteen
' frames in, and goes INTO block 8 - inside the wall - to be pushed back out
' two frames later when he comes down.  This records that.  Whoever ports
' CHECKBARR properly should find this scenario failing, and the answers it
' should then give are BUMPS 1 and BX 7.
SCEN a running jump still ends up inside the wall
AT 1 1 3 0
FACE 0
SEED 17
RUN ..>>>>>>>>JFFFFFFFF
WANT BUMPS 0
WANT BX 8
WANT ALIVE 1
END
